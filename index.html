Learn why endangered animals matter. Compete. Climb the leaderboard!

📜 GAME RULES

  • Each round has 15 questions about why endangered animals are important.
  • Single Player: Beat the clock! Your total time is recorded. Answer as many as you can correctly.
  • 2-Player Duel: Both players answer the same question on separate panes.
  • Answers are hidden until both players have submitted. Then the correct answer is revealed.
  • Each correct answer earns $10 base money.
  • Consecutive correct answers build a streak — each extra streak adds +$5 (e.g., 1st=$10, 2nd=$15, 3rd=$20…).
  • A wrong answer resets your streak to zero.
  • The player with the most money wins the duel!
  • Single Player scores are saved to the leaderboard. Same name replaces old score.
